# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Leisure travelers (20s-40s, solo or small groups) planning a trip quickly. They want a trustworthy, delightful plan.

## Product Purpose
TravelSynth turns origin, destination, trip length, budget tier, travel mode, interests and free-text notes into a day-by-day itinerary (Morning/Afternoon/Evening activities with place, duration, cost), stay recommendations and practical tips, generated by Google Gemini. Success: user goes from landing to a plan they would actually use, in under a minute.

## Positioning
Free, personalized per budget and interests, trips saved to the user's account (Firebase Auth + Firestore), printable as PDF.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Single-page React 19 + Vite + TypeScript app; views: landing, planner form, loading, itinerary, saved trips.
- Gemini API key via VITE_GEMINI_API_KEY; logic in src/utils/gemini.ts must not change.
- Auth: Firebase Authentication (Google + email/password). Signup records 18+ confirmation and ToS/Privacy acceptance in Firestore `users/{uid}`; trips in `users/{uid}/trips`. Legal pages at #/terms, #/privacy, #/cookies; cookie notice on first visit.
- Light and dark themes with toggle (localStorage `theme`).
- Print/PDF layout must keep working (`.no-print`, `.print-break`).
- Fonts pinned by the user: Bricolage Grotesque and DM Sans only.
- Existing SEO meta, JSON-LD, manifest and icons stay.

## Brand Commitments
Name: TravelSynth. Author: Ashutosh Swamy (footer links). Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque (display) + DM Sans (text), nothing else.

## Evidence on Hand
No testimonials, customer logos or usage numbers exist. Do not fabricate them. Templates in App.tsx (Paris, Tokyo, Swiss Alps) are real sample inputs.

## Product Principles
1. Plan first: the form and result are the product; marketing earns one click into them.
2. Honest claims only: no invented stats, users or reviews.
3. Fast and legible over ornamental; the itinerary must scan at a glance and print cleanly.
4. Accounts exist to save trips; say so plainly and never overclaim privacy.
